MindmetriQ practice

Browser remakes of the six Test Partnership MindmetriQ games, made for practice. Each game has a timed test, a 3-question practice round with feedback, and a harder drill. Finish a run to review every puzzle, see highlighted solutions, and compare accuracy by difficulty. Your scores are saved in this browser.

Numerical

Net the Numbers12 questions · 20 s each · drag the net to the highest sum Number Racer12 rounds · 5 s to plan · catch the numbers that sum to the target

Verbal

Link Swipe15 pairs · 6 s each · same, opposite, or other Word Logic15 analogies · 15 s each · three +10 s boosts

Logical

Pipe Puzzle10 puzzles · 20 s each · swap pipes to join the ends Shape Spinner10 wheels · 12 s each · same shape or same shading, not both

Unofficial practice remakes. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Test Partnership. All items are generated or written for this mock, and the scoring is a simple correct count, not the real Rasch/adaptive norm scoring.
