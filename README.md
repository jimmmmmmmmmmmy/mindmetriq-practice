# MindmetriQ practice games

These are unofficial browser remakes of the six Test Partnership MindmetriQ gamified assessments, made for personal practice. **They are not affiliated with or endorsed by Test Partnership.** Every item is generated or written for this mock. Scoring is a simple count of correct answers with a basic difficulty staircase, not the real adaptive Rasch norm scoring.

Open `index.html`, or any game file, in a browser. There's no build step and no server. The games work with touch, a mouse, or a keyboard, and your scores are saved in the browser's localStorage.

| Game | Items | Timer (taken from the official demo practice rounds) |
|---|---|---|
| Net the Numbers | 12 | 20 s per question |
| Number Racer | 12 | 5 s planning, then the numbers fall (about 14 s per round) |
| Link Swipe | 15 | 6 s per pair |
| Word Logic | 15 | 15 s per question, plus three +10 s boosts per test |
| Pipe Puzzle | 10 | 20 s per puzzle |
| Shape Spinner | 10 | 12 s per wheel |

The Link Swipe word bank is adapted from [Ryad2/Gamified_Assessment](https://github.com/Ryad2/Gamified_Assessment).
